A pinch you place by hand on a small round or slender object closes on air or slips within the first chunks of transport.

**Why:**
Your close lands on the near surface of a back-projected point and pinches a narrow part; it holds for the lift test and lets go under the policy's transport accelerations. The policy's own close, from a good approach pose, straddles the body.

**How to apply:**
- Put the open fingers above the object on a good line and let the policy make the close.
- If you close yourself, lift and look before you believe it, and expect the drop in the first chunk after handoff.

**Falsify:**
Hand-placed pinches on these objects surviving transport as often as policy grasps would remove this rule.

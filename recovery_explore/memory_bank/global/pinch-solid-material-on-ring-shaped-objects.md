**Why:**
Both control and treatment failed. Manual approaches, including a reported outer-edge attempt with changed pinch orientation, closed empty; a lower approach handed to the policy also produced another empty closure. These results do not establish that height alone caused the miss, nor that the fingers actually straddled solid material. A later return move overshot and was rewound. No successful grasp supports a preferred height or orientation.

**How to apply:**
For a ring-shaped target, test a grasp defined by the material between the fingers, rather than by the object's overall centre or a previous miss location. From a clear view, identify a solid rim segment and align the open fingers to straddle that segment before descending. Verify that the planned pinch crosses material rather than the central hole. If this geometry cannot be seen, expose it before attempting another close. Let the policy close from this visibly corrected pose, then check whether the object leaves its support. This is a proposed alternative, not a demonstrated solution.

**Falsify:**
If a clearly observed rim-straddling approach still closes empty or slips on lifting, while a centre-aligned grasp at corrected height succeeds, the proposed rim-targeting explanation is wrong or insufficient. A close without visible alignment cannot test it.

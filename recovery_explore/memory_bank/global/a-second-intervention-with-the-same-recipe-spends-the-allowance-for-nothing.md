After your own repair failed, a later intervention with the same recipe spends the allowance without changing the outcome.

**Why:**
In the runs without rewind, two thirds of intervened episodes were intervened again on the same failure, and the second and third plans were the first plan with a two- or three-centimetre change. They ended with the allowance exhausted and the policy left in a pose it had not chosen.

**How to apply:**
- A second intervention on the same failure changes category or does not happen: hand-placed grasp to policy grasp from a corrected pose, approach line to a different side, or leaving the policy alone with its remaining steps.
- When the first repair has failed, prefer retreat and an empty-plan fixed over a second correction from where the hand now is.

**Falsify:**
A same-recipe second intervention that rescued an episode the first one had not.

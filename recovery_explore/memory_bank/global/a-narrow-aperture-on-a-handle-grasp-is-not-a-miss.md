A mug or cup taken by the handle reads a few millimetres of aperture whether or not the handle is inside.

**Why:**
A handle is thinner than the empty-closure threshold, so the number cannot separate a handle grasp from a miss. In the rewound run the intervention called on the number was undone and the next window showed the mug rising with the hand.

**How to apply:**
- On a handled object, wait for the lift before calling a miss; a mug that rises with the hand was held.
- Do not reach in to regrasp on the width alone: that is how a run carrying its mug loses it.

**Falsify:**
A handle grasp that reads a wide aperture, or an empty close on a handle that reads wide.

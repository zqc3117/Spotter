Lift-test a stalled closed hand before opening it; near the object the stall can be a grasp the policy cannot carry out.

**Why:**
The same stalled mushroom grasp was met two ways: a three-centimetre lift with the fingers held brought the mushroom up and one hand-back finished the delivery; reopening the fingers dropped it, and the follow-up lift from that pose blew up.

**How to apply:**
- Near the object, treat a stalled closed hand as a possible grasp: lift three centimetres with the fingers held and look.
- Reopen only when the lift shows the object staying behind.

**Falsify:**
Stalled closed hands near the object that, when lifted, never bring the object with them.

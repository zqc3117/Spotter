Moving the hover point along the target's own long axis keeps the grasp on the target while putting the fingers further from the neighbour.

**Why:**
An elongated object offers a range of valid grasp points; the neighbour constrains only part of it.

**How to apply:**
- Two to three centimetres along the axis is usually enough.
- Prefer this over rotating the wrist, which costs more and changes the handoff pose.

**Falsify:**
Roughly spherical targets have no axis to slide along.

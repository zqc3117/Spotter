The first sight of a neighbouring burner lighting is not yet wrong-control failure; the policy often moves to the named knob within a window.

**Why:**
In the rewound run the wrong-control interventions were undone and the very next window showed the named knob's index moving under the policy's own hand. The hand-placed reseat on the correct knob, by contrast, closed on air or turned the neighbour again.

**How to apply:**
- Wait one window after the wrong control moves; act when the policy is still on the wrong knob a window later.
- When you do reseat, clear upward first and let the policy make the descent and turn; a hand-placed close on a knob mostly misses.

**Falsify:**
A stove episode where the policy stayed on the wrong knob to the end after lighting it.

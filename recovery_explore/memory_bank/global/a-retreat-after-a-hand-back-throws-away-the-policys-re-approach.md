A retreat after a policy hand-back throws away the re-approach the policy just made.

**Why:**
On the same cell the rewound run corrected the approach, handed back, lift-checked and handed back once more within one intervention and the avocado came out of the sink; the run without rewind made the same correction, handed back, then retreated the moment the hand-back had not finished, and repeated that recipe in two later windows without the object ever leaving the sink.

**How to apply:**
- After a hand-back that leaves the fingers near the object, the next step is a small lift with the fingers held, or one more chunk; retreat is not on the list.
- Retreat when your own steps failed before any hand-back, or when a hand-back ended with the fingers shut on nothing.

**Falsify:**
An episode where a retreat issued right after an undecided hand-back was followed by a better policy approach than continuing would have given.

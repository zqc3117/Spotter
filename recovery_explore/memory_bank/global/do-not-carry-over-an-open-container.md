A drop over a flat counter is recoverable. A drop into a sink or pot usually is not.

**Why:**
The cost of a drop depends entirely on what is underneath at that moment.

**How to apply:**
- Plan the horizontal path around open receptacles even if it costs one more action.

**Falsify:**
If the destination is the receptacle, crossing it is the task.

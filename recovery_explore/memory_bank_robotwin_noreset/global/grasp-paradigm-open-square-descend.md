**Why:** Reviewers watched repair after repair creep toward an object in one- and two-centimetre
steps with the fingers only half open and the wrist not squared; light objects were nudged away and
the closes came back empty. A grasp planned whole, from directly above with wide-open fingers, is
something the judge can do.

**How to apply:**
- Plan the grasp as ONE plan before moving: over the object (pixel move_to on its centre, a few
  centimetres up), open the fingers wider than the object, square the wrist to its narrow side,
  descend straight down, close, lift a little.
- Check in the pictures that the object came up; move on only then.
- If it did not come up, change the position or the orientation, not the depth by a centimetre.

**Falsify:** If grasps planned this way from directly above keep missing an object that is clearly in
reach, the aim point is wrong and re-aiming is the fix.

**Why:** A can was released before it was over the basket and landed beside it. Releasing is the
mirror of grasping and needs the same care about alignment.

**How to apply:**
- Carry the object to directly above the destination.
- Check in the head view and the wrist view of the working hand that it is squarely over it.
- Descend, then open the fingers, then lift away.

**Falsify:** If objects released while squarely over the destination still end up beside it, the
destination was misidentified.
